## Ideas

- One million robots in Norway by 2035, with robots as critical infrastructure. The government needs a robot strategy.
- Saro: physical AI hackathon in mid-January.
- NM i KI (the Norwegian championship in AI): one per cent of Norway's population is to take part.
- A large humanoid and robotics event in Norway in 2026. Robotics Norway supports this.
- Arrange robotics courses and webinars with Norwegian companies on robot policies, evaluations, hardware and similar topics, made available to members.
- Vidar, Lene and Eva will write a short text on what it takes to become a member, to be posted on the website.
- Services from suppliers: Lars-Fredrik and Lene will draft what the network can offer.
